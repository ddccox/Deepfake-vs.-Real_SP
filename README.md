This is an online repository made by Dianne Cosinas for MMS 200b: Special Project

Deepfake vs. Real is an educational multimedia platform designed to improve AI media literacy among Filipino Open and Distance e-Learning (ODeL) students through gamified video analysis challenges.

The rise of artificial intelligence (AI) has revolutionized how digital content is produced and consumed. Among its most controversial outputs are deepfakes, which are AI-generated videos, images, or audio that convincingly mimic real people or events (Copeland, 2025). While deepfake technology has potential uses in entertainment, education, and creative media, it has also been weaponized for harmful purposes, such as spreading political propaganda, fabricating news, committing fraud, and creating non-consensual explicit content (Momeni, 2024).
